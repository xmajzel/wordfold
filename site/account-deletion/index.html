WordfoldPrivacy policy

Delete a Wordfold account

Delete inside the app

  1. Open Wordfold Settings.
  2. Open Account and sync.
  3. Choose Delete cloud account and confirm.

This deletes your sign-in, synchronized collections, words, learning events, and private cloud assets. The app keeps a local vocabulary copy on that device and stops synchronization. Your Google Play lifetime purchase is not deleted.

Request deletion without the app

Email support@wordfold.app from the email address registered to your Wordfold account. Include only the account email address. Never send your password or vocabulary.

The account address will be verified before deletion. If you want to retain an up-to-date local copy, use the in-app deletion flow before uninstalling; an email request cannot create a new local copy on your device.

What may remain

Local data on devices is not remotely erased. Google Play and RevenueCat may retain purchase records where required for transaction, fraud-prevention, tax, or legal purposes. These purchase records do not keep your Wordfold cloud account active.
