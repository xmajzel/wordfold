Wordfold Privacy Policy

Effective 22 August 2026

Wordfold is provided by Jozef Majzel. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to support@wordfold.app.

Information Wordfold handles

Without an account, your vocabulary, translations, collections, learning progress, preferences, and reminders remain on your device.

If you choose to create an account and enable synchronization, Wordfold processes your email address, authentication identifier, collections, words, translations, and learning events through Supabase and PowerSync so that the data can synchronize between your devices.

If you are signed in, explicitly enable optional cloud neural pronunciation, and tap its sound control, the exact displayed word or phrase and selected locale are sent through a Wordfold Supabase server function to Microsoft Azure Speech. The resulting MP3 is stored in an account-private Supabase area and may be cached for your account on your device. Wordfold pronunciation metadata and audit records do not contain the raw word or phrase.

If you buy the lifetime unlock, Google Play processes the payment. RevenueCat processes product, transaction, entitlement, store-account, and technical identifiers needed to validate and restore the purchase. Wordfold does not receive your payment-card details and does not connect the RevenueCat identity to your Wordfold cloud account.

Cloud providers may process limited security and request metadata, such as timestamps, IP addresses, and error logs, to operate and protect their services.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide authentication, optional synchronization, optional cloud pronunciation, purchase validation and restoration, security, support, and account deletion. Wordfold does not contain advertising, does not sell personal data, and does not include a separate behavioral analytics SDK.

Service providers

Wordfold uses Supabase for authentication, database, storage, and server functions; PowerSync for synchronization; Microsoft Azure Speech for optional real-time speech synthesis; RevenueCat for purchase entitlement management; and Google Play for app distribution and payment processing. According to Microsoft, its real-time text-to-speech service does not retain the input text or generated audio. See Azure Speech data privacy and security. Each provider processes information under its own security and retention obligations.

Retention and deletion

Device data remains until you delete it or uninstall the app, subject to Android backup behavior. Synchronized account data remains until you delete your cloud account. Account-private pronunciation MP3s expire automatically 30 days after their latest use, and pronunciation audit records are retained for no more than 30 days. Device pronunciation files are cache data and may also be removed earlier by the operating system or Wordfold's cache limit.

Turning off cloud neural pronunciation immediately stops new cloud pronunciation requests and asks Wordfold to delete that account's saved private audio, pronunciation metadata, audit records, and local private pronunciation cache. If deletion cannot finish while offline, Wordfold keeps the feature off and offers a retry. The in-app account deletion flow also removes the Supabase account and associated synchronized and private pronunciation data while preserving a local vocabulary copy on that device. Purchase records may be retained by Google Play and RevenueCat where required for transaction, fraud-prevention, tax, or legal purposes.

Your choices and rights

Cloud neural pronunciation is optional and off until you explicitly enable it. Device pronunciation remains available without sending the word or phrase to Azure. You can use Wordfold without an account, choose whether to synchronize, turn off cloud pronunciation and delete its private audio, delete individual words, and delete your cloud account. Depending on your location, you may also request access, correction, restriction, portability, objection, or deletion by emailing support@wordfold.app.

Security

Cloud communication uses encrypted HTTPS connections. Access to synchronized data is restricted by authenticated account rules. No method of storage or transmission is completely risk-free.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, its effective date and published content will be updated before the changed processing is used.
