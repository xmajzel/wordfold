Wordfold Privacy Policy

Effective 13 August 2026

Wordfold is provided by Jozef Majzel. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to support@wordfold.app.

Information Wordfold handles

Without an account, your vocabulary, translations, collections, learning progress, preferences, and reminders remain on your device.

If you choose to create an account and enable synchronization, Wordfold processes your email address, authentication identifier, collections, words, translations, and learning events through Supabase and PowerSync so that the data can synchronize between your devices.

If you buy the lifetime unlock, Google Play processes the payment. RevenueCat processes product, transaction, entitlement, store-account, and technical identifiers needed to validate and restore the purchase. Wordfold does not receive your payment-card details and does not connect the RevenueCat identity to your Wordfold cloud account.

Cloud providers may process limited security and request metadata, such as timestamps, IP addresses, and error logs, to operate and protect their services.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide authentication, optional synchronization, purchase validation and restoration, security, support, and account deletion. Wordfold does not contain advertising, does not sell personal data, and does not include a separate behavioral analytics SDK.

Service providers

Wordfold uses Supabase for authentication, database, storage, and server functions; PowerSync for synchronization; RevenueCat for purchase entitlement management; and Google Play for app distribution and payment processing. Each provider processes information under its own security and retention obligations.

Retention and deletion

Device data remains until you delete it or uninstall the app, subject to Android backup behavior. Synchronized account data remains until you delete your cloud account. The in-app deletion flow removes the Supabase account and associated synchronized data while preserving a local vocabulary copy on that device. Purchase records may be retained by Google Play and RevenueCat where required for transaction, fraud-prevention, tax, or legal purposes.

Your choices and rights

You can use Wordfold without an account, choose whether to synchronize, delete individual words, and delete your cloud account. Depending on your location, you may also request access, correction, restriction, portability, objection, or deletion by emailing support@wordfold.app.

Security

Cloud communication uses encrypted HTTPS connections. Access to synchronized data is restricted by authenticated account rules. No method of storage or transmission is completely risk-free.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, its effective date and published content will be updated before the changed processing is used.
