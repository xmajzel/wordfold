## Operational interpretation

This permission authorizes commercial use of PCIC as a reference framework and the
use of relevant A1-C2 levels, categories, and classifications. It does not authorize
unrestricted reproduction of protected PCIC prose or other copyrighted materials.

Wordfold must:

- acknowledge PCIC when describing or presenting the curriculum structure;
- create or separately license every bundled headword list, definition, example,
  Slovak hint, and other learner-facing text;
- avoid certification, accreditation, endorsement, and official-validation claims;
  and
- avoid using the Instituto Cervantes logo or name as a promotional mark without
  separate written authorization.
