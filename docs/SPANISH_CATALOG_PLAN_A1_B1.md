# Spanish A1–B1: level-specific coverage planning

Status: proposed planning documentation, 2026-09-05. No learner-content authoring
or publication is authorized by this document. It supports the separate tasks
`plan-spanish-a1-complete-coverage-batches`,
`plan-spanish-a2-complete-coverage-batches` and
`plan-spanish-b1-complete-coverage-batches`.

## Exact disposition and closure rules

For each relevant PCIC subcategory, define an original objective and record one of:

- `covered-by-existing`: linked sense IDs, content hash and evidence showing that
  the selected meanings meet the objective; mere topic membership is insufficient.
- `new-sense-selected`: an enumerated sense with an owning cell and authoring work.
- `covered-by-cross-reference`: linked owning objective and sense IDs, without
  duplicating counts; the dependency must itself reach closure.
- `deferred-with-prerequisite`: named prerequisite and unresolved work. **A deferred
  required objective blocks full inventory completion. It cannot be hidden as an
  exclusion or counted as complete.**
- `not-applicable-with-rationale`: evidence that the subcategory is inappropriate
  for this level or outside the explicitly agreed version scope. This disposition
  needs independent coverage review; lack of authored content is not a rationale.

An owner-approved scope amendment may remove an objective from a later inventory
version, but cannot retroactively make unfinished required work complete under the
old version. Always preserve the version and disposition history.

Each sense-selection record contains lemma/MWE, POS, meaning gloss, proposed
level, prerequisite, objective IDs, original rationale, framework URL/section,
register/regional scope, morphology, pronunciation obligations, existing-ID match,
inclusion decision and unresolved uncertainty. Document exact bounds of closed
sets and number-composition patterns. For open domains, enumerate the chosen
scenarios and lexical contrasts, then obtain independent omission review.

Freeze only after every required objective has an adequate selection and every
placement/duplicate dispute is resolved. Calculate exact unique accepted sense
counts by level and cell. Split new/rewrite authoring deterministically into
shards of at most 50 senses; unchanged records receive audit coverage rather than
being counted again as newly authored words. All shard IDs and entry IDs must be
unambiguous and existing entry/sense identities preserved.

Catalog completion requires every frozen required objective and sense to reach
authoring, validation and independent Spanish/Slovak review closure, with no
unresolved mandatory findings. Corrections invalidate affected hash-bound reviews.
Preview eligibility and production release remain separate states.

## Legacy placement, collisions and boundaries

Individually audit the 500 legacy records, using ten stable 50-record chunks under
the master plan's review ceiling. Replace their generic category rationales with
meaning-level evidence and explicit retain/relevel/defer decisions. Audit the 185
supplemental placements against the final introduction/practice-level convention
as well. Reuse old review evidence only where its input hashes and scope match.

The PCIC A1–A2 specific-notion columns demonstrate why adjudication is necessary:
`firmar` appears in A1 personal information but is currently A2 in Wordfold; several
current A1 body-part terms appear in the PCIC A2 body column. These are evidence
for review, not instructions to automatically move a headword or every one of its
meanings. A chosen contextual use may need its own independently justified level.

Resolve at least these collision families before minting IDs:

- `tarde`: afternoon versus late; `este`: demonstrative versus east.
- `fuente`: fountain versus information source; `derecho`: right versus law.
- `salida`: exit versus departure; `consigna`: storage versus instruction.
- `recibo`/`ticket` and `salario`/`sueldo`: overlapping meanings/variants, not
  automatically duplicates or automatically separate instructional needs.
- `radio`, `televisión`, `reloj`, `enchufe`: decide useful sense granularity.
- Existing `buscar` versus its planned sense; prototype `pie` versus legacy `pie`.

Stable IDs contain level-like components. A placement change therefore requires
an explicit compatibility/alias decision for saved words and progress; do not
rename IDs or delete old records silently. Inflections, gender counterparts,
variants and genuinely different senses must follow the agreed counting policy.

## Pronunciation and morphology across every cell

Every entry must have written accent/stress review, applicable gender/plural and
irregular forms, pronominal/complement patterns and phrase-boundary checks.
For Slovak learners, review vowel quality versus vowel length, lexical stress,
Spanish `ñ`, tap/trill contrasts, silent `h` and spelling-to-sound patterns.
Dialect differences must be labeled rather than treated as errors.

- A1: intelligible word stress, basic sound contrasts, short questions/statements
  and usable isolated-word examples.
- A2: connected phrases, clitic/reflexive groups, diphthong/hiatus cases and routine
  sentence intonation.
- B1: meaning-preserving grouping/linking, prominence and narrative, complaint and
  question intonation.

Text review is not listening evidence. The physical-device gate verifies exact
`es-ES` routing, missing-voice/playback behavior and a risk-stratified listening
set. Any claim that particular audio assets are reviewed requires review of those
actual assets; representative device checks do not establish every utterance's
quality. AI reviewers must not sign as named native speakers. No paid synthesis,
remote deployment or EAS build is authorized by this plan.
