## Shared freeze and review rules

Each relevant subsection gets coverage by existing sense IDs, explicit new
selections, prerequisite cross-references or justified exclusions. Deferred core
coverage blocks completion; empty PCIC level cells do not require invented words.
Generic objective-name rationales in the 55 existing advanced next selections
must be replaced with sense/context-specific decisions.

Canonical ownership is lemma + POS + meaning/construction, not raw term.
Cross-topic repetition is a link; genuinely distinct meanings get distinct IDs.
Example: hesitant reserve versus rhetorical omission for reticencia could be
different meanings, but this is an adjudication question, not an automatic addition.

Every frozen row needs a gloss, communicative context, framework section/column or
independent evidence, lower-level comparison, borderline disposition, register/
region, forms, Slovak ambiguity notes and pronunciation risks. Derive counts only
after global reconciliation; split new/rewrite authoring shards at 50 senses.
Two independent reviewers inspect each final hash-bound entry; unresolved findings
block integration.

## Pronunciation across all advanced cells

Review written stress, variants, word/phrase boundaries and relevant forms for
every entry. B2 adds meaning-preserving sentence grouping; C1 adds contrastive
focus and deliberate pauses; C2 needs context-specific intonation and pragmatic
effects with regional alternatives clearly tagged. Written notes and es-ES device
availability do not prove spoken quality. Actual listening and native/device
evidence remain separately tracked under the launch policy.
